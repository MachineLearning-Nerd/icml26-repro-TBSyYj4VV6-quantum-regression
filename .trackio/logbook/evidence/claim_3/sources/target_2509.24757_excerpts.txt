We emphasize that our focus is on the computational task of approximately minimizing the loss function $F$ , rather than on the statistical learning problem of estimating an unknown model from random samples. The latter setting was studied by Chen and de Wolf ( 2023 ) , who investigated quantum algorithms for Lasso and ridge regression; in our notation, their results correspond to the case $m=O(\log n/\varepsilon^{2})$ .
