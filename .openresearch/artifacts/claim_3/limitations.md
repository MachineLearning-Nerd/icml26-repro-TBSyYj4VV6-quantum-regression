- The exact display counterexample remains scoped to the missing lambda.
- The preceding reduction strongly indicates that the missing lambda on the
  right is a typo. Reinstating it removes this counterexample.
- Firstness is separately falsified by primary prior art. This does not
  establish a lower bound against the target runtime or falsify a corrected
  non-firstness Lasso theorem.
- No numerical optimizer, tolerance, stochastic seed, or runtime formula is
  used; the contradiction is exact.
- The prior-art execution is a statevector query-model simulation with
  classical joining-time oracle construction. It corroborates that the
  pre-2025 source contains an executable quantum search path; firstness is
  falsified by publication order and objective equivalence, not simulator
  speed.
