# Claim 2 limitations and deviations

The falsification targets the exact proposed sparsification pipeline and its
all-epsilon quantifier. It is not a lower bound against every possible
quantum linear-regression algorithm. A repaired theorem could add
`epsilon=Omega(sqrt(n/m))` or specify a different dense-regime branch.
The statevector run constructs leverage values classically and does not
simulate the paper's leverage-score oracle. It directly executes the cited
quantum importance-sampling circuit; its finite objective result is
corroboration, not the universal falsification.
